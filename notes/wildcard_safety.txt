What * meant in my pattern:
The `*` wildcard matched any sequence of characters. It allowed me to work with multiple files that matched a particular pattern.

Why I listed matches before copying:
I listed the matching files first so I could confirm that the wildcard selected the files I actually intended to copy. This helped me avoid accidentally copying unrelated files.

Why wildcards are dangerous with delete commands:
Wildcards can match multiple files at once, so using them with delete commands can remove more files than intended. A small mistake in the pattern can cause important files to be deleted. I should always check the matching files before using a wildcard with a destructive command.
